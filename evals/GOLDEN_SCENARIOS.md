# Golden Scenarios

These are behavioral acceptance scenarios, not aesthetic guarantees.

## GS01 — 30s UGC product
Group same product entity; research only when useful/current; create audience/creative strategy; prefer reliable long generations over mechanical micro-shots; preserve product fidelity; show cost before paid calls.

## GS02 — Fashion ad
Learn/select camera, fit and fabric proof appropriate to fashion, not generic cinematic moves.

## GS03 — 60s xianxia drama
Three active characters, dialogue, action and cliffhanger. Persist identity/outfit/voice; use hierarchy story→blocking→camera; keep dependent segments sequential; compile Seedance-specific stages.

## GS04 — 120s Saturn sci-fi
Use factual research for Saturn/space realism when available; separate Fact Pack from creative strategy; create a mini-story rather than sightseeing; selective keyframes; mixed-media optimization allowed; no duplicate paid retry.

## GS05 — 7-character serialized film
Episode 1 uses only active subset. Episode 2 resumes canon without re-uploading all refs. Character knowledge state prevents omniscient dialogue.

## GS06 — Current-news explainer
Research required; Fact Pack with source provenance; no unsupported factual claims; production may prefer screenshots/charts/B-roll over all-generated video.

## GS07 — Multi-character dialogue
Preserve speaker/voice/eyeline/blocking and choose a route appropriate to model reliability.

## GS08 — External keyframe
Agent produces copy-ready prompt + ref pack, imports external image, validates it and continues without internal image generation cost.

## GS09 — Shot revision / Continuity Sandwich
Accepted Shot5→Shot6→Shot7. User: "Make Shot6 sword motion slower, keep face/outfit/light/end state." Keep v1, create v2, change only needed constraints, use incoming Shot5 state and outgoing Shot7 target, show incremental cost, and stale Shot7 only if necessary.

## GS10 — Spend/timeout safety
Potential upstream creation followed by timeout must not trigger blind second paid task. Reconcile upstream status and enforce spend cap.

## GS11 — Language change
Changing Vietnamese to Chinese after visual acceptance must not regenerate visuals unless lip-sync route truly requires it.

## GS12 — Product factual claim
Ad script must not invent unsupported product claims from an image.

## GS13 — 10-person battle
Planner must avoid exact overloaded choreography for all 10 in one shot; use readable wide/group + focused actions.

## GS14 — No-keyframe simple B-roll
Direct generation must work without forced storyboard/keyframe.

## GS15 — Provider switch
Switch provider exposing the same model with different API limits without rewriting Director logic.
