# Phase 4 — Long-form and Series

## Goal
Prove persistent narrative/visual/audio continuity across long projects and episodes.

## Focus
Project Canon, character/relationship/knowledge state, Voice Bible, scoped retrieval, episode commits, continuity baton, dependency scheduler, contextual revisions and artifact invalidation.

## Exit gate
- GS05 series scenario continues into Episode 2 without re-uploading canonical refs;
- characters do not leak audience-only knowledge;
- outfit/injury/prop/voice states persist correctly;
- retcon invalidates only dependent artifacts;
- long project context remains scoped rather than full-bible dumps;
- revised middle shot preserves or explicitly stales downstream continuity.
