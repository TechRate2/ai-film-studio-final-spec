# Dialogue Cinematography

## Retrieve when
An exchange changes power, knowledge or emotion. Match decision need, not a hardcoded niche branch.

## Decision
Choose shared two-shot, speaker or listener coverage according to who experiences the change; preserve listening.

## Trade-offs and when not to use
Do not cut on every line or hide crucial hands/eyelines in an over-shoulder foreground.

## Output and check
Record the chosen mechanism, reason and source skill version in the relevant CreativeStrategy, UniversalVideoSpec or timeline decision. Reaction timing and screen direction support the actual dialogue turn.
