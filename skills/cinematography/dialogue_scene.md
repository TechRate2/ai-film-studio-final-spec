# Dialogue Cinematography

## Retrieve when
An exchange changes power, knowledge or emotion. Match decision need, not a hardcoded niche branch.

## Decision
Choose shared two-shot, speaker or listener coverage according to who experiences the change; preserve listening.

## Trade-offs and when not to use
Do not cut on every line or hide crucial hands/eyelines in an over-shoulder foreground.

## Output and check
Record the chosen mechanism, reason and source skill version in the relevant CreativeStrategy, UniversalVideoSpec or timeline decision. Reaction timing and screen direction support the actual dialogue turn.

Read the linked knowledge only for the missing decision. Capability/rights/locks/canon and spend rules outrank this heuristic. No failed aesthetic check authorizes paid regeneration.

## Coverage and audio feasibility
Map each line to speaker, listener, tactic and change in knowledge/emotion. Compare shared framing with reaction coverage; avoid alternating close-ups mechanically. Keep eye axis, hands and any concealed prop visible when relevant. Check chosen dialogue language, approved speech windows and whether mouths are visible before choosing native or controlled voice. An unchecked additional lip-sync option never authorizes hiding faces, changing staging or claiming mouth match; surface the trade-off under spec/09.
