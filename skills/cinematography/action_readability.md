# Action Readability

## Retrieve when
An action beat loses geography or cause. Match decision need, not a hardcoded niche branch.

## Decision
Choose a clear travel axis and one dominant causal change; use a wide to orient and a detail/reaction only for a consequential event.

## Trade-offs and when not to use
Avoid decorative movement during exact contact; excessive fragmentation hides who did what.
