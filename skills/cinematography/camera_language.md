# Camera Language

## Retrieve when
A beat needs framing or movement decisions. Match decision need, not a hardcoded niche branch.

## Decision
Choose information/emotion/power function, compare a locked frame, then specify a motivated start/path/end.

## Trade-offs and when not to use
Avoid simultaneous orbit/zoom/pan without a demonstrated need; close views can remove necessary geography.

## Output and check

## Applied decision contrasts
Stable proof: preserve the product/hand/action without a cut that hides the mechanism.
