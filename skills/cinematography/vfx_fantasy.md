## Retrieve when
A supernatural event needs readable cause and scale. Match decision need, not a hardcoded niche branch.

## Decision
Define emitter, path, impact and changed state, then simplify camera or effects if coordination is unproven.

## Trade-offs and when not to use
Do not increase effects to compensate for missing stakes; excessive simultaneous events raise omission risk.
