# Selective Keyframe Policy

## Retrieve when
Visual anchoring may improve a risky shot. Match decision need, not a hardcoded niche branch.

## Decision
Decide whether an anchor is useful, then select existing/user/external/previous/internal source under capability and cost policy.

## Trade-offs and when not to use
Do not call ImageProvider while merely deciding need or fallback from a failed external upload silently.

## Output and check
Record the chosen mechanism, reason and source skill version in the relevant CreativeStrategy, UniversalVideoSpec or timeline decision. The selected route meets locks; NONE/external reuse has zero internal image submission.

Read the linked knowledge only for the missing decision. Capability/rights/locks/canon and spend rules outrank this heuristic. No failed aesthetic check authorizes paid regeneration.
