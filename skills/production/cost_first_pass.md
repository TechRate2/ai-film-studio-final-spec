# Cost / First-Pass Skill

## Retrieve when
Expensive generation has avoidable uncertainty. Match decision need, not a hardcoded niche branch.

## Decision
Compare reuse, still+motion, continuation and generation; spend bounded preflight effort where expected acceptance improves.

## Trade-offs and when not to use
Unmeasured probabilities are not numbers; endless planning and mandatory keyframes can cost more than they save.

## Output and check
Record the chosen mechanism, reason and source skill version in the relevant CreativeStrategy, UniversalVideoSpec or timeline decision. A feasible route, bounded spend and explicit acceptance evidence support the choice.
