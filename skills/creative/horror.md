# Horror Skill

## Retrieve when
Uncertainty or anticipated threat drives attention. Match decision need, not a hardcoded niche branch.

## Decision
Control what is seen/heard; let negative space and a delayed causal sound create expectation before reveal.

## Trade-offs and when not to use
Do not use constant jolts or camera shake; if geography is unclear suspense becomes confusion.

## Output and check
Record the chosen mechanism, reason and source skill version in the relevant CreativeStrategy, UniversalVideoSpec or timeline decision. Threat knowledge is intentionally distributed and the reveal changes the situation.
