# Product Demo Skill

Primary objective is product truth + visual proof. Identify which benefit must be visible, which claim needs FACT_SOURCE, which angles/details prove material/shape/fit/function, and which reference is HARD_LOCK. Do not hide product under decorative VFX or invent unseen features. Hook may be problem, transformation, tactile detail, comparison or use-case proof; choose from audience/evidence rather than a fixed template.
