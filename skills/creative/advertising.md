# Advertising Skill

## Retrieve when
The objective is a persuasive action. Match decision need, not a hardcoded niche branch.

## Decision
Identify awareness, objection and needed proof; connect an honest promise to visible value and a feasible CTA.

## Trade-offs and when not to use
Do not force pain-solution-CTA on every brand film; unsupported claims and fake proof invalidate persuasion.

## Output and check
Record the chosen mechanism, reason and source skill version in the relevant CreativeStrategy, UniversalVideoSpec or timeline decision. The audience can state the benefit, its evidence and the intended action.

Read the linked knowledge only for the missing decision. Capability/rights/locks/canon and spend rules outrank this heuristic. No failed aesthetic check authorizes paid regeneration.
