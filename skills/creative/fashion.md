# Fashion Skill

## Retrieve when
Silhouette, fit or garment movement is central. Match decision need, not a hardcoded niche branch.

## Decision
Use body framing for fit, a controlled turn for drape and selective detail for finish; preserve reference design.

## Trade-offs and when not to use
Constant cuts hide drape; low light or stylized deformation can misrepresent color and fit.

## Output and check
Record the chosen mechanism, reason and source skill version in the relevant CreativeStrategy, UniversalVideoSpec or timeline decision. Required garment details remain readable and no fabric claims are invented.
