# Drama Skill

## Retrieve when
A scene changes a relationship or choice. Match decision need, not a hardcoded niche branch.

## Decision
Give each active character objective, obstacle and tactic; stage the change through action and listening.

## Trade-offs and when not to use
Avoid exposition characters cannot know and escalation that only increases volume.

## Output and check
Record the chosen mechanism, reason and source skill version in the relevant CreativeStrategy, UniversalVideoSpec or timeline decision. The scene leaves a different knowledge, relationship or decision state.
