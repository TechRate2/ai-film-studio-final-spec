# Editorial Rhythm

## Retrieve when
The sequence needs motivated cut/hold decisions. Match decision need, not a hardcoded niche branch.

## Decision
Cut when attention or meaning changes; retain reaction/recognition time and contrast density with breath.

## Trade-offs and when not to use
Uniform duration, constant montage and cutting every line can erase performance.

## Output and check
Record the chosen mechanism, reason and source skill version in the relevant CreativeStrategy, UniversalVideoSpec or timeline decision. Cuts have stated purpose and no necessary reaction or geography is lost.
