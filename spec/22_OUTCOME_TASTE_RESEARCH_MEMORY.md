# Outcome, Taste and Research Memory

## UserTasteProfile
Learn from accepted/rejected versions, revision text, chosen hooks/concepts, preferred camera/acting/VFX density and recurring voice/style choices. Persist each learned dimension with evidence count, confidence, source signals and version. Do not overfit from one isolated choice.

Taste is a lower-priority grammar layer. Explicit project constraints, canon, product truth, brand hard rules, safety/rights and verified evidence outrank learned taste.

## Outcome learning
When analytics exist, store retention/watch-time, CTR, completion, engagement and conversion where applicable. Connect outcomes to creative decisions/DecisionRecords rather than only final files.

## Model learning
Aggregate scenario, prompt/spec/compiler structure, duration, active character count, ref count/type, acceptance, failure reason, cost and latency. Update ModelProfile recommendations only from sufficient evidence and keep provider/version scope.

## Research memory
Stable evidence is reused within freshness scope. Provider/model/current evidence expires or invalidates by version/date/source rules. CreativeEvidenceGraph may connect evidence to observed outcome without turning correlation into universal truth.

## No weight training initially
Learning is retrieval/memory/benchmark driven; production does not require model weight training to become useful.
