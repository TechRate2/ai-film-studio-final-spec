# Evidence and Verification Policy

Evidence labels:
- `VERIFIED_SOURCE`: code/docs directly inspected
- `REPO_REPORTED`: repository/project claim
- `PRODUCT_OBSERVED`: behavior observed in product/testing
- `SYNTHESIS`: architecture/design conclusion
- `UNVERIFIED`: current commercial/provider claim not live verified

See `/evidence/PUBLIC_SOURCE_MAP.md`.

Evidence demonstrates patterns; it does not prove private studio implementation details. Provider/model facts must carry freshness and scope. Seedance 2.5 behavior must not inherit Seedance 2.0 measured bias without verification.
