# Adaptive Research Intelligence

## Principle
Do not invent when evidence can cheaply improve a material decision. Do not search again when fresh, high-confidence evidence already exists.

## Research router
Evaluate independently: factual/current, trend/viral, niche/audience, film/genre/cinematography, model/provider, prompt strategy and reference-driven research.

## Workflow
```text
Understand request
→ identify high-impact uncertainty
→ ResearchPlan
→ collect sources
→ rank/dedupe
→ verify contradictions/freshness
→ EvidencePack
→ Director decision
```

## Evidence hierarchy
1. official model/vendor docs
2. official repositories/skills
3. controlled/public benchmarks
4. credible repeated specialist/community evidence
5. isolated examples/opinions

Community claims may inform experiments but do not silently become capability truth.

## EvidencePack
Required concepts: facts, best practices, model capabilities, prompt tactics, failure risks, successful patterns, conflicting findings, source confidence, freshness and provenance.

## Research budget
Configurable max queries/sources/refinement/cost; stop when decision confidence is sufficient.

## Research memory
Key by `model + provider + task + niche/genre + generation_mode`, with `last_verified_at`, source version, provider scope and TTL/invalidation.

## Creative research
Extract mechanisms rather than copying expressive content: hook mechanism, pacing, camera rhythm, proof/reveal structure, emotional progression, CTA/payoff and audio/edit rhythm.

## Opening–Body–Ending intelligence
For each section ask: why stop, why care, why believe/feel, what payoff, why continue/remember/act?
