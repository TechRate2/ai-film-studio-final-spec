# Performance, Cinematography, Audio and Editorial Direction

## Performance & Blocking
Plan body positions, entrances/exits, gestures, eyelines, screen geography, distance/power relation, pauses/breaths, reaction targets and subtext. Avoid static "A talks/B talks" scenes.

## Cinematography
Camera choices serve information, emotion, power relation, action readability, scale, reveal and continuity. Do not equate cinematic quality with random orbit/dolly/zoom.

## Audio Director
Timeline may include dialogue, ADR, narration, ambience, foley, SFX, music and silence. Audio state participates in continuity.

## Editorial Rhythm Brain
Decide cut points, holds, reaction shots, J/L cuts, match action, eyeline match, audio bridges, montage, transition language, subtitle rhythm and music energy. FFmpeg/Remotion executes decisions; it is not the decision-maker.

## Context-driven coverage decision
For each beat choose the information/proof/emotion change, subject relationship, necessary visible hands/objects, speech/mouth requirement and continuity axis before camera technique. Compare a held frame against a motivated move/cut. Record purpose, alternative rejected, start/end composition, subject versus camera motion, cut/reaction rationale, observed success cue and complexity risk in existing CreativeStrategy/DecisionRecord/UVS fields. No niche field may directly force tripod, handheld, orbit, close-up or a fixed number of cuts.

Use contrasting fixtures: a product mechanism needing an uninterrupted hand demonstration may favor a stable medium/detail view; a confrontation may favor a two-shot and a held listener reaction; travel through a room may need a motivated reveal; action may need a wider body/prop view. Then swap the intent while holding the niche label fixed, and hold intent fixed while changing niche labels. The decision must follow purpose, evidence, user camera locks and measured route capacity. 'Cinematic' must not increase moves or paid candidates by itself. Multi-angle editorial coverage can come from one qualified multishot generation, approved existing media or justified separate generations, never one paid call per panel by default.
