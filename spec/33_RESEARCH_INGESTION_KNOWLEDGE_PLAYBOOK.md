# Research Ingestion and Knowledge Playbook

## Goal
Continuously improve the Director from real evidence without turning random internet claims into product truth.

## Research decision
Estimate value of information: material uncertainty × consequence of wrong choice × evidence availability versus search cost/latency. Skip research when fresh trusted knowledge is already sufficient.

## Source processing
Collect → canonicalize source → extract claims/examples → rank credibility → detect conflicts → attach freshness/scope → distill to EvidencePack → store reusable evidence.

## Capability claims
Official provider/model docs and controlled probes outrank community tips. Community patterns can seed probes or creative hypotheses but remain scoped/confidence-labeled.

## Creative pattern mining
From successful reference/viral content extract transferable mechanisms: opening mechanism, audience promise, narrative information flow, shot duration distribution, camera function, performance pattern, audio rhythm, proof/reveal/CTA. Do not copy protected expressive content.

## CinematographyReferenceMiner
For reference videos extract shot scale, camera height/path, lens feel, subject motion, screen direction, approximate shot duration/cut points, lighting/composition and relation to narrative beat.

## Model/prompt research memory
Key evidence by model version + provider exposure + scenario + generation mode + resolution/duration where relevant. Store verified date and invalidate when provider/model changes or benchmark contradicts prior knowledge.

## Stop condition
Research must stop when incremental evidence is unlikely to change the decision materially. Endless browsing is a product defect.
