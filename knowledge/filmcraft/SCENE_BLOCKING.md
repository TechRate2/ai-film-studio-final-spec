# Scene Blocking

Plan where bodies are, what they are doing and who/what they look at before writing camera moves.

Track entrances/exits, screen side, distance, elevation/power, eyelines, hands/props, orientation, movement direction and reaction targets.

Dialogue behavior should include physical actions, pauses and reactions rather than static alternating speech. Example: character crosses to table, sets sword down, avoids eye contact, then answers; listener reacts before the camera cuts back.

For AI generation, simplify simultaneous action when reliability is low. Preserve one readable dominant action/change per stage where practical.
