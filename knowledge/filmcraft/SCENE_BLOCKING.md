# Scene Blocking

Evidence class: SYNTHESIS — reusable directing heuristics, not measured provider behavior.

Draw a mental floor plan before lens choice: entrances, exits, obstacle, key prop, who can see whom and the action axis. Track world position separately from screen position; a camera reversal can change screen side without moving a person. Use eyeline target IDs and relative height/direction instead of “looks left” without a reference frame. Maintain hand/prop ownership, contact, occlusion and finish pose. A handoff has giver possession → contact → receiver possession; the object must not teleport or duplicate.

Distance and orientation can carry subtext: approach to seek agreement, turn away to conceal, hold ground to refuse. Stillness is valid performance. Do not force walking or gesturing into every dialogue line. Motivation precedes movement and listening precedes a reaction. A crossing of the axis is legitimate if the move or a neutral/geography shot reorients the viewer; otherwise a reverse angle can reverse the perceived pursuit or opponent.

For multiple characters choose a focal exchange and let others support through grouped reactions, depth or off-screen sound. Do not assign equal close-up coverage to everyone. When exact contact choreography is unreliable, keep the same causal action but change coverage at an occlusion, impact or reaction that preserves geography. Record entry/exit entity states and required coverage, not a mandatory count of generated clips.
