# Composition and Lens Language

Use composition to direct attention and express relation/scale.

- wide/establishing: geography, isolation, spectacle, group relation;
- medium: performance/action balance;
- close-up: reaction, detail, intimacy, proof;
- low/high angle: power/vulnerability only when story supports it;
- negative space: expectation, isolation, unseen threat;
- foreground layers: depth and observation without random clutter.

Describe lens **feel** when exact focal length is not useful to the generator: compressed/observational, natural perspective, close immersive wide, shallow/deep focus. Avoid fake technical precision that does not improve model adherence.

Maintain screen direction and eyeline across dependent cuts unless breaking them intentionally.
