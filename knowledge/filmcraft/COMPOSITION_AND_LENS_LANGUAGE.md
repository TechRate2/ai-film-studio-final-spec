# Composition and Lens Language

Evidence class: SYNTHESIS — reusable directing heuristics, not measured provider behavior.

Choose subject priority first. Scale controls information: a wide clarifies geography and body relationships; a medium preserves hands and expression; a close view isolates a decision/detail but loses context. Camera height changes access and perceived relation; low angle is not automatically powerful and high angle is not automatically weak. Compare what the viewer gains and loses with each framing. Use negative space only when expectation, off-screen presence or a graphic target needs it.

Perspective depends on camera position; lens feel and distance work together. For generative models use visible spatial relationships and depth rather than pretending a focal-length number guarantees optics. Deep focus may be essential for two planes of action; shallow focus may hide product proof or listener response. A rack focus should transfer attention to a story-relevant target, not merely advertise technique. Screen-safe framing accounts for subtitles/overlays and target crop without hiding important hands, faces or labels.

Movement must change information, relation or feeling. Pan follows a reveal, track maintains relation to action, push-in concentrates attention, pullback exposes isolation, orbit can shift allegiance/geography but adds continuity risk. Pick one dominant path with start/end framing; do not stack contradictory movement verbs. A locked camera is the baseline alternative. Preserve the intended axis and horizon unless disorientation is a motivated, brief choice followed by reorientation.

## Coverage choices by visible purpose
A trustworthy review is a purpose, not a camera preset. To prove a latch works, preserve the hand, latch and opening in the same readable space; a stable medium or close detail may outperform a floating hero shot. A separate insert is useful when the detail cannot be read, but an intervening cut may weaken evidence that one continuous action happened. Avoid shallow focus when it hides the mechanism or invented macro details on unseen surfaces. Conversely, a review showing movement through a space may need a controlled walk-through; do not force tripod from the word 'review'.

In a dramatic confrontation, establish who stands where and what each wants. A two-shot preserves distance and mutual reaction; a closer listener view can reveal changed belief without another spoken line. Shift angle/scale at a change of tactic, discovery or power when it adds meaning. More angles do not automatically improve a film. If the reveal depends on a hand concealing an object, keep that hand readable rather than defaulting to a face close-up. Native or controlled speech constraints must be planned with visible mouths and eyelines, not patched by hiding faces after cost approval.

For action, show the preparation, direction, contact/consequence and recoverable geography. Separate subject movement from camera movement. A restrained follow may preserve a sword path better than a simultaneous orbit and zoom. For suspense, a held composition/negative space can let the audience anticipate an entrance; avoid a premature camera reveal. For an establishing scale shot, a motivated pullback may reveal isolation, but not if it loses a product detail that is the beat's real purpose. These patterns can combine across genres; retrieve by decision need.

## Generation-aware prompt tests
Express a held camera positively with stable position/framing while describing the actor's actual movement. A camera route specifies starting composition, meaningful path and ending composition; a coverage route specifies cut motivation and continuity anchors. Do not request both an unbroken take and separate reverse-angle edits. Use measurable visibility and end-state cues to judge the result: label legible, hand-to-prop contact observable, listener response held, geography understandable. 'Looks cinematic' is insufficient acceptance evidence. The exact phrase effectiveness and affordable stage count remain provider-scoped empirical questions.
