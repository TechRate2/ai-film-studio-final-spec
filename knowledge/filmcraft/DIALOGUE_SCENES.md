# Dialogue Scenes

Dialogue is performance plus listening, not only text.

For each turn define speaker, listener, intention, subtext, emotion, pace, pause, eyeline and physical behavior. Reactions can carry more story than another line.

Use shot/reverse-shot, two-shot, over-shoulder, profile or single close-up only when they serve relationship/power/information. Preserve eyeline and screen side.

For multilingual projects, keep canonical meaning/personality separate from localized wording. Voice identity is separate from language. If model-native dialogue is unreliable, preserve visuals and route controlled voice/post audio rather than regenerating the whole scene unnecessarily.
