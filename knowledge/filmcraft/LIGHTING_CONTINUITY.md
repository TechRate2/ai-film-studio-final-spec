# Lighting Continuity

Track dominant light direction, source type, time/weather, exposure mood, color relationship and practical sources across dependent shots.

Canonical visual identity is not only face/clothing: a sudden unmotivated lighting direction or day/night shift can break continuity.

When references conflict, explicitly prioritize the canonical scene lighting while limiting identity refs to identity. Previous accepted output can guide observed continuity, but must not silently redefine canonical character identity or product appearance.
