# Lighting Continuity

Evidence class: SYNTHESIS — reusable directing heuristics, not measured provider behavior.

Establish motivated sources and their relation to subject/world: key direction, softness, intensity relationship, practicals, time/weather and color intent. Choose visibility for the dramatic or proof function. A low-key look can support uncertainty but is wrong when it hides a required product feature; flat even light can prove material but reduce dramatic separation. Specular surfaces may need controlled reflection rather than simply more light.

Separate source color from product/skin identity. A colored scene light can tint appearance without canonically changing an outfit. On a reverse angle preserve world light position; “light from frame left” in every shot may contradict the room. Track exposure and contrast through accepted observations, allowing motivated transitions such as a character entering shadow or opening a door. Reference backgrounds/lighting do not override locked scene lighting merely because they are visible in identity photos.

Compare adjacent accepted frames for dominant source, shadow direction, face readability and material color. Unknown off-screen lamps are not invented facts. If a deterministic grade can correct a mild mismatch without destroying skin/product truth, consider it before a paid revision. Severe directional/geometry errors may require a new video plan; QA diagnoses and requests authorization, never automatically regenerates.
