# Series Story Logic

Evidence class: SYNTHESIS — reusable directing heuristics, not measured provider behavior.

Track dramatic causality through facts, choices, relationships, resources and costs. Each major recurring character needs an objective and changing strategy; not every episode must advance every arc. Select active cast by causal need and preserve off-screen state without filling the prompt with the entire registry. A shared world event may be audience-known but disclosed to characters at different times.

Use proposition IDs and acquisition events to distinguish world truth, rumor, deception, suspicion and deliberate concealment. Before a reveal, check who can witness it; after it, update only those knowledge edges. Flashbacks use prior story positions, not current omniscient snapshots. Alias collisions remain unresolved rather than creating duplicate characters or merging distinct people. Track unresolved promises with origin, relevance and possible payoff; avoid perpetually adding hooks that never resolve.

Episode acceptance commits an immutable snapshot with source versions, accepted media, current injury/outfit/prop/voice state and unresolved threads. A summary points to structured facts; it never replaces them. A retcon preserves history and selectively invalidates consumers of the changed fact, including later episodes and exports. Reverts can restore compatibility but require checks rather than blind CURRENT flags. Long-single films use the same causal scene memory without forcing episode wrappers.
