# Sound Design

Evidence class: SYNTHESIS — reusable directing heuristics, not measured provider behavior.

Choose an auditory point of view and foreground priority per beat. Dialogue carries words; ADR repairs or re-records speech; narration frames information; ambience establishes place; foley supports physical contact; designed SFX communicates exceptional action; music shapes expectation; silence removes a layer intentionally. These are independently editable stems when available. Do not request every layer loudly in the native generation prompt.

Place sound causally: onset at action/contact, plausible perspective and decay, continuity across cuts. A room-tone bridge can hide a technical cut but must not imply the wrong space/time. Music energy can rise through harmony, texture or register without louder volume or faster cuts. Duck music/ambience around critical speech with explicit envelope/sidechain timing; aggressive ducking pumps unnaturally. Silence may mean no score while room tone persists, or deliberate full silence—declare which.

Choose native audio when exact route quality and language are proven; choose controlled speech when identity, wording and pronunciation dominate. Mixed native audio is not automatically separable into clean stems. Assess leakage/artifacts before claiming audio-only repair. Verify timing, voice, pronunciation, clipping, loudness and missing/duplicate ranges against explicit export policy. Platform loudness rules and rights must be verified when used; do not invent a universal target. Repair mix/timing deterministically when possible and preserve accepted visual sources.


For original-video dubbing (spec/47), listen for intention, breath, emphasis, relationship and turn-taking before choosing delivery. Preserve a hesitant answer or uncomfortable silence when it carries meaning; filling every gap with translated speech can erase performance. Prefer natural target phrasing that preserves the factual and emotional beat, then test it against the available turn. Do not compress an accusation's crucial negation or a product's number to fit. Subtitle phrasing can be shorter than spoken phrasing without changing those anchors.

Use modest, policy-bounded tempo adjustment only while words and vocal character remain intelligible. Overlap, laughter, singing and damaged separation may need focused human choices. Do not claim clean background from a noisy extraction or emotionally faithful acting from a speaker similarity score. In the original-video-only workflow, preserve the existing pictures and explain that timing alignment does not alter mouths; do not retrieve the generative dialogue coverage/lip-sync suggestions as automatic repairs. New TTS after a result remains a paid user-directed revision.
