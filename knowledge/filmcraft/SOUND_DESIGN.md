# Sound Design

Audio layers: dialogue/ADR, narration, ambience, foley, designed SFX, music and intentional silence.

Use audio to establish place, continuity, tension and editorial bridges. Silence can be a designed event.

Track music/ambience state in continuity when it crosses shots. Avoid restarting ambience or score arbitrarily at every generated clip boundary.

When native model audio is useful, preserve it selectively; when voice control/consistency matters more, combine generated ambience/action sound with controlled voice in post. Never regenerate accepted visuals solely because a replaceable audio layer is weak unless sync requirements truly demand it.
