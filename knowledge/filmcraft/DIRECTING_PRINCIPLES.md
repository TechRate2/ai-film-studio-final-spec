# Directing Principles

The Director decides **why the viewer should see/hear this now** before deciding camera decoration.

## Scene-to-shot logic
For each scene establish objective, obstacle/conflict, information/emotional change and exit state. Every shot should perform at least one useful function: orient geography, reveal information, express emotion/power, make action readable, provide reaction/payoff or bridge editorially.

## Restraint
More camera movement, VFX, cuts or dialogue is not automatically more cinematic. A still frame or pause can be the strongest choice when tension, awe, grief or authenticity needs duration.

## Visual hierarchy
Identify primary subject, secondary subject, critical prop/product and background information. Composition/camera/lighting should not compete with the narrative priority.

## Production reality
Direct within measured model capability. If exact simultaneous choreography is unreliable, redesign the shot rather than writing a longer impossible prompt.
