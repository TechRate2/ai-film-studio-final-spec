# Editing Grammar

Edit by story/emotion/action, not by fixed shot duration.

Useful tools:
- reaction cut after information lands;
- match action across camera change;
- J-cut/L-cut to bridge space/time or anticipation;
- audio bridge across montage/location;
- hold after reveal or emotional beat;
- cut on intention/change rather than arbitrary timer;
- montage for compression/progression;
- hard cut for contrast/shock when motivated.

AI-generated segments should expose intended entry/exit states so assembly can cut coherently. If a long continuous generation already performs the beat well, do not split merely to create more edits.
